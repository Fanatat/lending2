# Planet textures

(https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) and are distributed under
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

They were downscaled and recompressed for the web (sizes 512–2048 px,
JPEG q72–80; the cloud map converted to grayscale). No other changes.
